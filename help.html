Primary, secondary and higher

When you register you choose your section. Afterclass then sets itself up for it, and each section only sees its own tools, levels and paper styles. Higher accounts can switch between university, polytechnic and college under School. Registered in the wrong section? Use Report a problem and we'll move your account.

  • Primary: pupils don't need accounts. Lessons start with Class teams on the board. Each class has a star chart you tap to award stars, and Start a new term resets it. Questions are easy by default. Printed papers are a short Class Test with "Circle the correct answer". Class codes and the class leaderboard are hidden.
  • Secondary: students register and join a class with its code. Use live games, CBT tests, the class leaderboard, practice and WAEC/NECO-style exam papers.
  • Higher (university, polytechnic, college of education, training): classes are called courses and students use their matric number. Live sessions turn on audience Q&A, and the dashboard lists your course codes.

Primary: the Learning Corner and class tablets

The Learning Corner has more than 60 learning games for Primary 1–6, all read aloud with big buttons and Mbe the Tortoise:

  • Numbers: counting, ten-frames, adding and taking away with pictures, story sums, times tables, fractions, telling the time, naira money, shapes, patterns.
  • Letters and words: letters, first sounds, spelling with letter tiles, rhymes, tricky words, plurals, grammar, capital letters.
  • Science, Our Nigeria, Art and Hausa, Igbo and Yorùbá numbers and greetings.
  • Stories (Nigerian folk tales read aloud), memory pairs, letter and number tracing, a drawing pad and rhymes.

Questions get harder after three right answers in a row and easier after misses, so every child works at their own level.

  1. On the board: on Home, press Learning Corner and play with the whole class.
  2. On class tablets (no codes for pupils): open your class and press Set up a class tablet. Scan the QR code with the tablet once. Pupils then tap their own picture, choose an animal and play. Their stars land on your star chart, and the class page shows what each pupil practised and which subject needs more work.
  3. Live games on tablets: when you run a live game with the class list, a big "Your teacher's game is on!" button appears on the tablet. Pupils join with one tap, with no PIN to type.
  4. At home: parents can open the Learning Corner on any phone. Nothing is saved there.

Your first live game (about 5 minutes)

  1. Sign in at Teacher and open Quizzes. No quiz yet? Choose + New quiz ▾ → Sample quiz to get a ready-made one.
  2. Press ▶ Play live on the quiz, choose the time per question, then Open host screen.
  3. Put the host screen on a projector or TV, or share it on Zoom / Google Meet. It shows the web address, a QR code and a 6-digit game PIN.
  4. Students open the address on their phones (or scan the QR code), type the PIN and their name. You can also press Share on WhatsApp to send the link to your class group.
  5. When everyone has joined, press Start. After each question you'll see how the class answered and a leaderboard; at the end, a podium.
  6. Results are saved under Results, where you can download them for Excel.

No phones in class? Choose More ways ▾ → Class teams: the question goes on the board and you score the teams.

Record a lesson, get questions

  1. Open Afterclass in Google Chrome (Android phone or computer). Go to Quizzes → + New quiz ▾ → Record a lesson.
  2. Press the red button and teach as normal. Your words appear on the screen as you speak. Keep the page open; the screen stays on by itself.
  3. Press Stop & make questions. The text opens in the quiz editor: correct any words that came out wrong, then press Generate questions.

Tips: put the phone close to you or use a clip-on microphone; say key points clearly, as you would for notes ("Osmosis is…", "The three types of … are …", "… because …"); tell your class you are recording. No audio is saved, only the text. If the phone locks or the page closes, your words so far are kept and you can continue.

Fact check and short notes

  • When you press Generate questions, Afterclass first checks well-known Nigerian facts in your notes: dates (independence, republic, amalgamation, the naira, Abuja), the number of states and local governments, the National Pledge, anthem, flag and motto. If something looks wrong you'll see the correct fact with a Fix button.
  • If your notes are too short, press Expand my notes → Expand from what I taught. Afterclass reads your lesson, lists the points you made in your order, and adds only the facts that match each point, from checked notes and from your own earlier lessons (the notes saved with your quizzes). You see what will be added before anything changes.
  • You can also add a whole checked topic note (for example the National Pledge, Socialisation, Citizenship, Matter) with one tap.
  • With the AI writer turned on, Check facts checks every subject and Expand with AI turns a few lines into full lesson notes.

A class test or assignment

  1. On the quiz choose More ways ▾ → Assignment / online test.
  2. Pick a class list (so each student gets one attempt and results show real names), an optional timer and a closing date.
  3. Share the link or PIN (WhatsApp works well). Students who registered and joined your class see the test on their dashboard automatically.
  4. Written answers get a provisional mark; confirm them in Results.

Class lists and class codes

  1. Open Class lists, create a class (e.g. "JSS 2A") and paste names and admission numbers from Excel or your register.
  2. Each class has a class code. Students register on the home page, enter the code and are added to your list.
  3. Tests you set for the class then appear on their dashboards, and the class leaderboard fills up.

Print an exam paper

  1. Set up your heading once under School: search for your university, polytechnic or college to fill in the name and motto, then upload your logo.
  2. On a quiz press Print. Choose the paper style, sections, marks and number of versions (Type A–D).
  3. Press Print / Save as PDF, or Download Word file to edit it further. A marking scheme and answer sheet are included.

For students

  • To join a game you only need the PIN: no account.
  • Register on the home page to get a dashboard: your teacher's tests, your results, the class leaderboard and Practice, where you can replay quizzes you've taken.

Install Afterclass on your phone

  • Android (Chrome): open the site, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
