Privacy notice

Afterclass is used by schools, lecturers and event organisers to run quizzes, tests and polls. The school or organiser who gave you the PIN is responsible for your data (the "data controller") under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect when you join

  • The name or nickname you type, and your admission / matric number if your teacher uses a class list.
  • Your email address, only if the organiser asks for it (for example for a certificate).
  • Your answers, your score and the time you answered.
  • For timed tests: how many times you left the test page. This is shown to your teacher as a hint only.
  • Questions you post in Q&A (shown to everyone in the session without your name).

We do not collect your phone number, location, contacts or photos, and you do not need an account.

Who can see it

Only the teacher or organiser who created the session. Other students see only what appears on the shared screen (nicknames and scores in live games, unless the teacher hides scores). Written answers are shown on the big screen without names.

How long it is kept

Results are deleted automatically after the period the school chooses (one year unless they set it shorter). Teachers can delete results at any time.

Your rights

You can ask your teacher or the organiser to see, correct or delete your answers and details. For children, a parent or guardian can ask on their behalf.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, a cloud database provider, on servers that may be outside Nigeria, protected by encryption and access rules so that each teacher can only see their own sessions.
