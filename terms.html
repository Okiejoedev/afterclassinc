Terms of use

Afterclass helps teachers, lecturers and trainers make quizzes, tests and exam papers, and helps students practise and take them. By creating an account or joining a session you agree to these terms. They are written to be short and plain.

Accounts

  • Give your real name when you register. Teachers are responsible for what they put in their quizzes; students for the answers they submit.
  • Keep your password private. If you think someone else is using your account, change your password and tell us.
  • Students under 13 should use Afterclass with a teacher, parent or guardian, and may join games with a PIN without creating an account.

Acceptable use

Do not use Afterclass to:

  • post or ask questions that are abusive, sexual, hateful, or that bully or embarrass anyone;
  • upload material you don't have the right to use, or other people's personal information;
  • cheat in a test, take a test for someone else, or try to get answers before a test closes;
  • pretend to be a school, examination body or person you are not, or print papers in an institution's name without its permission;
  • try to break, overload or get around the site's security.

We may hide content or close accounts that break these rules.

Your content

Quizzes, notes and questions you create remain yours. You let Afterclass store and display them so the service works (for example, showing a quiz on your students' phones). Logos and crests you upload must be ones you are allowed to use.

Results and data

Teachers and schools decide how long results are kept and can delete them at any time. How personal data is handled is explained in the privacy notice.

The service

Afterclass is provided as it is. We work to keep it available and correct, but cannot promise it will never be interrupted. Automatically written questions and provisional marks must be checked by the teacher before they count. Keep your own copies of important exam papers and results (you can download them).

Problems and changes

Use Report a problem at the bottom of any page to reach us. If these terms change, the new version will be posted here.
